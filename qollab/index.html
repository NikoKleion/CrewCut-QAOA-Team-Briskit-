Crew assignment with QAOA

Twelve repairs on a damaged feeder go to two crews. Each repair is one qubit: A for crew 1, B for crew 2. The circuit samples plans from a pair cost with angles trained in advance, and every shot is scored on the true cost (weighted completion time). The backend is the one picked in Select QPU.

Pick the settings, then press Run the circuit. IonQ backends take one job per run.
